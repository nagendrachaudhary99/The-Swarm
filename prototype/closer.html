Nobody here has a name.

Closer shows you what people around you are saying right now. Five rules, then you're in.

1

The map shows places, never people

Rooms, gates, the library steps — those are drawn, named and glowing. People are only ever a distance. There is no dot for a person anywhere in this app.

2

Distance, never direction

Every post says how far away its writer is, rounded to ten metres. You are never told which way, so you can't walk towards anyone. Finding someone isn't blocked — it's information the app never has.

3

Walk into a room to talk in it

Each place on campus is its own channel. You can only post to the room you're actually standing in, and rooms nobody has posted from go dark — light one up and it's worth triple.

4

Only you can reveal yourself

Tap reveal on your own post and your name appears on that one post. Nobody can ever do it for you, and no amount of walking closer will do it either.

5

Glow is what you give

People brighten posts they like, and that light goes to whoever wrote it. Go quiet and it fades. It resets every dawn, so nobody builds a permanent reputation.

6

Your inbox keeps the same strangers

Reply to anyone and that thread stays. They get a two-word label so you can tell your conversations apart, and you can pick what they call you — a different name for every person if you want. None of it shows on the map or on a post. Nothing joins them up.

7

Circles: your own anonymous groups

Make a group for anything — your floor, a subject you hate, the people awake at 3am. Share the four-letter code out loud and whoever you give it to gets in. Inside a circle you get a two-word alias so people can follow what you say — and it only exists in that circle. The same person is a different stranger in every group.

8

Report anything, block anyone

Every post has a ⚑. Reporting is anonymous in both directions — they are never told, and you never learn who they were. Three reports hide a post immediately. Blocking works even though nobody has a name: the server knows who wrote it and simply stops showing you their posts, without telling either of you anything.

If something is wrong

Post it as HELP. It reaches the whole campus instead of one room, stays up for half an hour, and everyone can tap I'm coming — so you can watch help move toward you without any of you knowing who the others are.

How should they know you?

Only this person sees this, and only inside this one conversation. It never appears on a post, on the map, or in any circle — and you can use a different name for every person you talk to.

THE UNLOCK

Places are public, people are not

This is what makes a real map possible. Rooms can be drawn, named and lit up because a room isn't a person. Your location only ever leaves as a number: how far.

THE GAME

Light the dark rooms

Every room nobody has posted from tonight is drawn dashed and dim. First person to speak there gets triple glow. It turns your campus into a board you fill in by walking around it.

THE GAME

Carry a post

Pick up something you love and carry it to another room. It arrives marked as carried, not written. Good lines travel across campus in people's pockets.

THE POINT

Fear is easier without a name

The things people most need to say — being followed, being harassed, being unwell — are hardest to say with your name on them. Take the name away and they get said.
