Safety is the mechanic

  • Coordinates never leave the server. The client asks “how far?” and gets back a band, not a point. ST_DWithin does the work in Postgres.
  • The 10-metre floor. Under 10 m the ring pops and returns nothing, forever. There is no zoom level that finds a chair.
  • The freeze right. Anyone being tracked is told, and standing still dissolves their signal. Being found is always a choice.
  • Mutual consent to reveal. No face-off, no DM, no photo unless both sides tap yes in the same minute.
  • 24-hour amnesia. Whispers, pings and location rows are deleted on a cron job, not archived.
  • .edu gate + rate limits. One verified school, 30 pings/hour, three tracks/day. Scarcity doubles as abuse control.

The loop

1

Nightly. Dusk to dawn, every day, so it becomes a habit. Glow resets at sunrise — nobody carries a reputation into tomorrow.

2

Ping. No feed, no scroll. Energy is finite, so every look costs something and nothing is passive.

3

Reveal. Whispers decay in ~22 seconds. Miss it and it is gone — that is the FOMO engine, not a notification badge.

4

Glow. Boosts brighten you. Silence dims you back into the dark. Status is visible, earned nightly, and lost nightly.

5

Hunt. The ring gets you to the room and then pops. You look up from your phone at real people. That is the moment they screenshot.

◉

Blooms. Finals, Holi, Diwali, first monsoon, first snow. Six nights a year the palette, particles and rules all change. Daily habit, rare peaks — the push that says HOLI BLOOM IS LIVE is the growth strategy.

The two brakes — keep these or it burns out in three weeks

1. The night ends. Three finishable rituals, then the app tells you it is done and stops asking. Every product that removes the ending gets uninstalled in anger; the ones that keep it get opened for years. Wordle has one puzzle a day for a reason.

2. The streak has a shield. One missed night is absorbed automatically. A streak should be a memory of a habit, not a hostage — the moment it punishes people they start resenting the number, then the app, then quit at exactly the moment they were most invested.

Schema · Supabase + PostGIS

create extension postgis;

create table whispers (
  id          uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  author      uuid references auth.users,          -- never sent to clients
  body        text check (char_length(body) <= 140),
  geog        geography(point) not null,           -- never sent to clients
  klass       text check (klass in ('bard','rogue','mage')),
  boosts      int  default 0,
  dies_at     timestamptz default now() + interval '22 seconds'
);

-- the only geo the phone ever sees: a band, never a point
create function band(w uuid, me geography) returns text as $$
  select case
    when ST_Distance(geog, me) < 10  then 'mirage'   -- ring pops, nothing returned
    when ST_Distance(geog, me) < 60  then 'hot'
    when ST_Distance(geog, me) < 180 then 'warm'
    else 'cold' end
  from whispers where id = w;
$$ language sql security definer;

select cron.schedule('reap','* * * * *',
  $$delete from whispers where dies_at < now()$$);
